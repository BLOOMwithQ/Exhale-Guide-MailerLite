Before you record

How this was built — and how to voice it

These are seven nightly tracks, one per day of The EXHALE. Each is written to be spoken at the end of the day, in the dark, at roughly 110 words a minute — unhurried, close to the microphone, like you are the last calm voice she hears before sleep. Every night opens differently on purpose, so it never feels like a template repeating; the arc moves from noticing the body to finally letting it rest.

Record them in one sitting while your voice is warm. The pacing marks below are written the way ElevenLabs reads them — leave them in the text field exactly as shown.

The Seven Nights
Before Night One

The Welcome

~1:30
Track 00
DirectionThe first voice she hears. Unhurried and intimate — you are meeting her, not performing for her. Smile on the opening line; she should hear it.

Hi, sis.<break time="1s" /> I am so glad you are here.

If you are listening to this, some part of you already knows the truth — that you have been carrying more than anyone has ever thanked you for. That you have been the strong one for so long you forgot you were allowed to be soft.<break time="1.5s" />

So here is what the next seven nights are, and what they are not.

They are not a program. There is nothing to complete, nothing to get right, no one grading you. This is simply a few quiet minutes at the end of each day — my voice, and permission to put the whole thing down.<break time="1.5s" />

One reflection a night. That is all. Listen in bed, in the bath, in the car after everyone has gone quiet. You do not even have to keep your eyes open.<break time="1.5s" />

We will begin gently — with breath. And by the seventh night, something in you will have shifted. Not because you worked at it. Because you finally stopped.

So take one slow breath in with me now… and let it go.<break time="2s" />

Welcome to the exhale, sis. Let's begin.

Softly, to closeWhen you are ready… press play on Night One.
Night One

Breathe

~2:00
Track 01
DirectionArrival. She has just put the day down. Slow the very first line almost to a whisper — you are lowering the lights with your voice.

Wherever you are right now — you can stop.<break time="1.5s" />

The list is not going anywhere. The people you love are safe. For the next two minutes, there is nothing to fix and no one to carry.

Take one slow breath in… and a slower breath out.<break time="2s" />

All day you have been proving you are alive by how much you did. Tonight, notice you are alive by how it feels to simply be here.

Ask, gently — not what did I accomplish today, but… how was I alive today?<break time="1.5s" />

You do not have to answer well. You only have to ask.

“I refuse to confuse survival with living. Tonight, I choose to be fully alive.”

Softly, to closeRest now, sis. I will be here again tomorrow.
Night Two

Rest

~2:10
Track 02
DirectionPermission. She feels guilty for stopping. Warm and certain — you are giving her something she has been waiting to be told.

Let me tell you something no one told you.<break time="1s" /> You are allowed to be tired.

Not because you failed. Because you have been the well the whole village drinks from — and no one thought to ask who was refilling you.<break time="1.5s" />

Somewhere you learned that your exhaustion was proof of your goodness. Tonight we set that down.

Resting is not withdrawing from the people who need you. It is protecting the source they need.<break time="1.5s" />

So breathe out the thing you have been postponing until you are “less busy.” It will keep. You matter more.

“I am not abandoning my responsibilities. I am restoring the source from which I meet them.”

Softly, to closeClose your eyes, sis. Nothing tonight requires you.
Night Three

Trust

~2:10
Track 03
DirectionRelease. Her mind is still managing everyone. Steady, grounding — help her set weight down, one thing at a time.

Before you sleep, let us put some things down that were never yours to hold.<break time="1.5s" />

You have carried other people's moods. Other people's choices. Outcomes you were never in charge of. And you called it love.

But watching everything so closely is not love. It is fear wearing love's coat.<break time="1.5s" />

What is actually yours? Your integrity. Your peace. Your becoming. That is the list. Everything else, you can loosen your grip on tonight.

Picture your hands opening in the dark.<break time="1.5s" /> Whatever was never yours — let it go where it belongs.

“I release what was never mine, so I can faithfully care for what is.”

Softly, to closeYour hands are empty now, sis. Sleep with them open.
Night Four

Receive

~2:00
Track 04
DirectionSoftening. She is better at giving than taking. Tender, almost coaxing — you are teaching her hands to open upward.

You are so good at giving.<break time="1s" /> Tonight, I want to teach you the harder thing. Receiving.

Somewhere you decided everyone else's needs deserved a faster answer than your own. That the help, the rest, the tenderness — those were for other people.<break time="1.5s" />

What have you been turning down because it felt undeserved?

Hear this clearly: you do not have to earn care. A field does not earn the rain.<break time="1.5s" />

Let tonight's rest be the first thing you receive without apology. You cannot pour from a cup you refuse to let anyone fill.

“I receive without apology, so that I may give without depletion.”

Softly, to closeLet it be given to you tonight, sis. Just this once, take it.
Night Five

Beauty

~2:00
Track 05
DirectionNoticing. Quieter, more spacious. Let the images breathe — the peony, the ocean, the sunrise. Do not rush the pictures.

No one applauds the peony when it opens.<break time="1.5s" /> The ocean does not wait for a crowd before it catches the light.

Beauty was never a reward for finishing everything else. It is not the prize at the end of the to-do list. It is oxygen.<break time="1.5s" />

Somewhere you began treating it as optional — the candle, the good sheets, the flowers only bought when someone was watching.

Ask this as you drift: if my home and my body were given the same care I give everyone else… what would soften first?<break time="2s" />

You do not cultivate beauty because life got easier. You cultivate it because you are alive.

“I refuse to postpone beauty until life is easier. I make beauty because I am alive.”

Softly, to closeLet the room be lovely while you sleep in it, sis.
Night Six

Bloom

~2:10
Track 06
DirectionBecoming. The emotional peak — still quiet, but full. A little more voice here. This is the night she stops asking permission.

The rose never asks permission to open.<break time="1.5s" /> It does not wait to be sure it is wanted. It simply becomes what it already is.

Where in your life have you been asking for a permission that was never actually required?<break time="1.5s" />

What version of you have you postponed — kept folded up and quiet — for someone else's comfort?

Tonight you stop negotiating with your own becoming. You do not have to be perfect to begin. You only have to be alive.<break time="1.5s" />

You have spent so long being the rock. Sleep tonight as the rose.

“I no longer negotiate with my own becoming. I choose to bloom — not when I am perfect, but because I am alive.”

Softly, to closeYou are already blooming, sis. Rest inside it.
Night Seven

The Declaration

~2:20
Track 07
DirectionThe keepsake. Warm, close, a little ceremonial. This night gathers all six — and opens the door to what comes next. End almost in a whisper.

Seven nights ago you were still the rock.<break time="1.5s" /> Tonight, listen to how far you have come.

You remembered how to breathe. You let yourself rest. You set down what was never yours. You learned to receive. You made room for beauty. And you began to bloom.<break time="2s" />

None of that was a project. It was a returning — to the woman who was here all along, underneath everyone's needs.

This is your declaration, and you get to keep it:<break time="1s" />

“I belong to myself before I belong to the world. I choose to be alive. And I am allowed to rest.”

But here is the truth about rest, sis — it is not a single week. It is a practice you come back to, night after night.<break time="1.5s" />

So I am not saying goodbye. I am saying… I will meet you here again tomorrow night.

Softly, to closeRest well, sis. This was only the beginning.
The Bridge · Optional Outro

…and this is where it becomes Rest Well, Sis.

Record this once and append it to Night Seven — or keep it aside as the opening of your nightly series. It turns a seven-day guide into a ritual she never has to leave.

If these seven nights gave you something — do not let them end here.<break time="1.5s" />

The exhale was never meant to be a one-week event. It is a place to come home to.

Every night this can be yours: a few quiet minutes, my voice, and permission to put the whole day down.<break time="1.5s" />

It is called Rest Well, Sis. And it is simply this — the last calm voice you hear before sleep, waiting for you whenever you need it.

So for tonight… nothing more is required of you.

Rest well, sis. I will be right here.
